About

I'm currently pursuing B.E. in Computer Science & Engineering, where I split my attention between two things that pull in the same direction: making software people can trust, and making software people enjoy using. That means I'm as likely to be tightening an encryption routine as I am refining a button state in Figma.

My work so far spans cybersecurity awareness tools, AI-assisted applications, and interface design — built on the belief that a good product has to be both secure and genuinely usable, not one at the expense of the other.
